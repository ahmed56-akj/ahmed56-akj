## About Me

I'm a Full Stack Developer and AI Engineer based in Hyderabad, Pakistan, with 4+ years of experience building web applications, AI-powered systems, and automation tools for clients and personal projects.

I work primarily with **React, Next.js, Node.js, and Python**, and I focus on shipping applications that are functional and reliable — not just demos. Alongside standard web development, I integrate **OpenAI and Claude APIs** into products, build **Telegram and WhatsApp automation**, and develop machine learning applications for real use cases.

I help businesses and founders turn ideas into working software: full stack apps, SaaS platforms, internal tools, APIs, and automation systems that save time and reduce manual work.
